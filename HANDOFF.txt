FINDINGS ABOUT THE MODEL (assets/models/base_female/model_0.obj, used by Oto, Chi-Chi, Bulma, civilians)
  - The head is a blank smooth egg: no eyes, nose, mouth or relief at all. About 0.137 H wide and
    0.196 H tall (head spans y 0.80..1.00 of body height; chin ~0.80, widest ~0.89). Eye line chosen
    at 0.881 H (1.50 m on the 1.7 m model); head radius at eye line ~0.14 m.
  - The landmarks.json head ellipsoid (head_c/head_r/ry/rz) is only roughly right: the real mesh is
    fuller at the top. The hair "long" style (cap ball set BEHIND the head plus blocky side slabs and
    ONE white bang slab on the +X side) leaves a bare forehead and no hairline, and the bang covers
    part of her left eye region. Hair "long" is used ONLY by oto and oto_stage, so it is safe to
    rebuild: a hair shell that conforms to the real head (ray-cast, like the face) with a fringe and
    the loose strand of bangs the design doc asks for, kept off the eyes.
  - Skin is chocolate brown (0.29,0.16,0.09) (ModelLoader "oto"). Black ink barely reads on it, so
    the eyes need visible sclera, pale brows (hair is pale grey) and plum lips. In Shadow forms Player
    tints the skin 55-75% toward black (FORM_TINT) and the face must follow.

DESIGN DECISIONS (from the design doc and the owner's references)
  - Eyes: "very dark, so dark they seem to be looking at something just behind you": near-black
    violet iris, tiny dim catch-light placed outer-upper, gaze PARALLEL or slightly outward (iris_dx
    ~ +1 mm) so she looks past the viewer. Style: start from "almond" (Android 18 / Mai family:
    calm, cool) softened toward tall_oval warmth ("warm on top, cold underneath"). Heavy upper lid
    line with a wing and two lash flicks, faint crease, thin lower line.
  - Brows: "calm", pale grey-lilac (0.6,0.58,0.64) to read on dark skin; BLACK in Shadow forms.
  - Mouth: "still" (deadpan dash, tiny lower lip). She does not fidget: slow rare blinks, every 5.5-9 s.
  - Shadow forms: "void" variant (black irises, no catch-light, black brows); deeper forms (2-3)
    use the "cold" look (narrow eyes, flatter brows). Never gold, never glowing.
  - Nose: small "button" 3D bump (skin material) + a soft under-nose mark; chin "soft" (none).

THE ARCHITECTURE TO FINISH (agreed; none of this is written yet)
  1. scripts/characters/face.gd  (class_name Face)
     - LOOKS: character recipes {eyes: style+tune, brows, mouth, nose, chin, palette overrides,
       blink interval}; "oto" first. EXPRESSIONS: named combos of eye/brow/mouth states
       (neutral, cold, smile, surprise, sad, eat, sleep).
     - Texture builders with a static cache keyed look|layer|state|variant|skin: eyes_texture
       (draw LEFT eye, flip_x for the right, blend_rect into a layer image; if gaze != 0 draw the
       right eye separately with -look), brows_texture, mouth_texture, nose_texture. Layer image =
       Image(px rect of the layer region) filled Color(skin, 0); canvases blend in at
       canvas.rect.position - layer.rect.position (mirrored x = TEX_W - (x + w)).
       Add a static FaceCanvas.px_rect(region) helper (same rounding as _init).
     - Separate LAYERS (eyes, brows, mouth, nose marks), each its own transparent plate mesh and
       texture, so blinking or a mouth change swaps ONE small texture. Regions (mm): eyes
       (-120,-52,240,122); brows (-120,6,240,96); mouth (-52,-116,104,66); nose (-22,-66,44,40)
       (Rect2 x,y,w,h). They cover the left-side canvases FaceParts draws and their mirrors.
     - Plate mesh = ray-cast heightfield wrapped on the real head: TriangleMesh from
       head_mi.mesh.generate_triangle_mesh() (cache per model); rays from the head axis outward at
       angle theta and height y; u linear in theta (x_mm = theta * R_REF, R_REF = 140), v linear in
       y; vertex = hit + normal * ~1.2 mm; normals from grid finite differences (dTheta x dY);
       triangles [TL,TR,BR, TL,BR,BL] (Godot front faces are clockwise); head axis z = midpoint of
       front and back hits at the eye line. Wrapper space = rig.transform * head_mi.transform
       (rig = visual.get_child(0); head part is the child named "head" if present, else child 0).
     - Material: StandardMaterial3D, albedo_texture, TRANSPARENCY_ALPHA, roughness 0.8, mipmapped
       anisotropic filter, MeshInstance cast_shadow OFF; small emission on the eyes layer.
     - 3D nose/chin = ellipsoid MeshInstances using the HEAD'S OWN skin material object
       (head_mi.material_override), so skin tint, Shadow-form darkening and the Void flicker follow.
  2. scripts/characters/face_rig.gd  (class_name FaceRig extends Node3D), added under the Outfit
     root as "Face": holds layers, current states, look and skin. API: express(name), set_part(slot,
     state), start_talking()/stop_talking() (mouth cycles ah/oh/ee/wide/closed about every 0.09-0.14 s,
     never the same viseme twice; CLOSED otherwise), blink() (open>half>closed>half>open, about
     0.05/0.08/0.05 s, skipped when eyes are already closed/happy), set_form(form, tint) (void
     variant, albedo tint like the skin, keep TRANSPARENCY_ALPHA always), _process for timers.
  3. Outfits: preset key "face": "oto" on oto and oto_stage; Outfits.apply attaches after b.build,
     skin colour = head material's base_color meta; set visual meta "face" = the rig.
  4. Player._apply_form_visual: call the rig's set_form(form, FORM_TINT[form]); the form-2 flicker
     loop (player.gd ~line 431) must also fade the face layer materials. Do NOT put the face
     materials in the "mats" meta: that loop turns transparency off in forms != 2.
  5. Overlay (scripts/overlay.gd, say() types at 45 chars/s): add `signal talking(speaker: String,

  6. Gallery: add `--gallery faces` (flat parts sheet over a skin-coloured background: every eye

  7. Hair: rebuild "long" to sit on the real head with a hairline and fringe (see findings).
  8. Tests (scripts/tools/dev_tests.gd): every eye/brow/mouth style and state draws with no error
